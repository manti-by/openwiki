# <PROJECT_NAME> Wiki — Update Log

OKF v0.2 log for the bundle: a flat, date-grouped history of wiki changes,
newest first. One `## YYYY-MM-DD` heading per day, with `* **Creation**:`,
`* **Update**:` and `* **Deprecation**:` entries. Maintained by `/wiki-sync`
(one entry per page write) plus `/wiki-dedup` and `/wiki-archive` (merges and
retirements). Entries are prose; the bold lead word is a convention, not a rule.
